# Syncali copy rules

How Syncali writes. These apply to the website, the auth pages, plugin notices,
release notes, and error messages. If a sentence would fail one of these rules,
rewrite the sentence.

## The voice in one line

**A competent friend explaining their own tool, not a company selling to you.**

Dry, warm, specific. Confident enough not to shout. The reader is smart and
busy — respect both.

## 1. Say what it does in the first ten words

Someone landing cold should know what this is before they scroll. No riddles,
no "reimagine your workflow."

> ✅ Sync your Obsidian vault across devices, encrypted, on your own server.
> ❌ The future of knowledge management is here.

## 2. Be concrete. Numbers beat adjectives

Specifics are more persuasive than superlatives, and they're checkable.

> ✅ Changes land on your phone in about three seconds.
> ❌ Blazing-fast sync.

> ✅ 1,331 notes is about 3 MB. You will not run out of room.
> ❌ Generous storage.

## 3. Dry wit, never zany

One raised eyebrow per page. Humour comes from being honest about something
slightly awkward, not from exclamation marks or emoji.

> ✅ Runs on Cloudflare's free tier. We are as surprised as you are.
> ❌ Sync your notes at LIGHTNING SPEED! 🚀🚀

## 4. Second person, active voice

Write to one person. "Your notes", never "users' notes".

> ✅ You hold the key. We could not read your notes if we wanted to.
> ❌ Vault contents are encrypted prior to transmission by the client.

## 5. Never oversell security

This app holds people's private notes. Say exactly what is protected and what
is not. Overclaiming here is worse than a boring sentence.

> ✅ Your notes and file paths are encrypted on your device. File sizes,
>    timestamps and how much you store are not.
> ❌ Military-grade encryption. 100% secure.

## 6. Own the tradeoffs

Credibility comes from admitting limits before the reader finds them. Every
honest limitation you name buys trust for the claims you make.

> ✅ There is no collaboration. One account, your devices. That is the whole
>    product.
> ❌ Enterprise-ready collaboration coming soon!

## 7. Cut the filler

Banned outright: **seamless, effortless, revolutionary, game-changing,
cutting-edge, robust, leverage, empower, unlock, supercharge, delight,
best-in-class, simply, just**.

"Simply" and "just" are the worst of these — they tell a stuck reader the
problem was easy, which only makes them feel stupid.

## 8. Buttons are verbs

Label the action, not the concept. "Create vault", not "Vault creation".

## 9. Errors say what happened, what it means, and what to do

An error the reader cannot act on is a bug in the copy.

> ✅ Could not write "Why Planning is Important?.md" — Android does not allow
>    `?` in filenames. Rename it on your computer and sync will continue.
> ❌ Automatic sync failed: FILE_NOTCREATED

Name the file. Name the cause. Name the fix. In that order.

## 10. Credit Synch

Syncali is a fork of [Synch](https://github.com/hjinco/synch) by hjinco, MIT
licensed. Say so plainly wherever the project is described. Never imply we
wrote the original, and never imply Synch endorses this fork.

## Sentence mechanics

- Short sentences. If it has two commas, it is probably two sentences.
- Cut adverbs. "Very fast" is slower to read than "fast".
- Contractions are fine. This is speech, not a contract.
- One idea per paragraph. Three lines maximum on the website.
- British or American spelling — pick one per surface and hold it. The website
  is American.

## The test

Read it aloud. If you would not say it to someone at a table, rewrite it.
